# Compensation Bands 2026 (CONFIDENTIAL)

Managers only. Do not share.

## Engineering bands

- Engineer I: EUR 18,000 to 24,000
- Engineer II: EUR 24,000 to 32,000
- Senior Engineer: EUR 32,000 to 45,000
- Staff Engineer: EUR 45,000 to 60,000

## Review cycle

Salary reviews happen once a year in March. Managers submit recommendations to HR by 15 February.
