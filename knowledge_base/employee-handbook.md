# Employee Handbook

Welcome to Kestrel Digital. This handbook covers how we work day to day. For leave, expenses and IT, see the dedicated pages.

## Working hours

Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like, as long as you work your contracted hours and your team knows when to reach you. Put your working pattern in your Slack profile.

## Remote and hybrid work

Kestrel is hybrid. Teams agree their own office days, but most teams are in the Skopje office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. During your first two weeks we recommend coming in at least three days so you can meet people. Working from abroad for more than 10 working days a year needs HR approval in advance.

## Probation

Probation lasts three months. You will have check-ins with your line manager at Day 30, Day 60 and Day 90. The Day 90 meeting confirms the end of probation.

## Your human Buddy

Every new joiner gets a Buddy: a colleague outside their direct team who helps with the unwritten rules. Your Buddy is your first stop for "is this normal?" questions, introductions and anything about team culture. Your Buddy will book a 30-minute coffee with you in week one and a check-in every week for your first month.

## Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Concerns about behaviour should go to your line manager or directly to HR at people@kestrel.example. You can also raise concerns anonymously through the Speak Up form on the intranet.
