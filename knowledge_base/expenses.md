# Expenses Process

## What you can claim

You can claim reasonable costs incurred for work: client travel, approved training, and client meals. Home office equipment is covered by a one-off home office budget of EUR 300 in your first six months. Personal software subscriptions are not reimbursed unless pre-approved by your manager.

## How to submit a claim

Submit claims in Spendly (spendly.kestrel.example) within 30 days of the purchase. Photograph the receipt in the Spendly mobile app or upload a PDF. Choose the correct category and add the client or project code (for example HALO-001) in the description.

## Who approves expenses

Claims up to EUR 500 are approved by your line manager. Claims over EUR 500 need a second approval from Finance (Arben Hoxha). Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run if approved before the 20th of the month.

## Company card

Company cards are only issued to people who travel regularly. Ask your manager if you think you need one.
