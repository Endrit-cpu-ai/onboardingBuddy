# Glossary of Acronyms and Codenames

## Acronyms

- **The Bench**: engineers who are between client projects. Bench time is used for internal tools, training and pre-sales prototypes.
- **SOW**: Statement of Work. The contract document describing what we deliver for a client project.
- **T&M**: Time and Materials. A billing model where the client pays for hours worked, as opposed to fixed price.
- **UAT**: User Acceptance Testing. The phase where the client tests a release before go-live.
- **PDR**: Personal Development Review. Our twice-yearly development conversation with your manager (not a performance rating).
- **OOO**: Out of office.
- **ICR**: Internal Change Request. The form used to request changes to internal systems or access, submitted in Jira under the ICR project.
- **DoD**: Definition of Done. The checklist a Jira ticket must meet before it moves to Done.
- **TLC**: Tech Lead Council. The monthly meeting of tech leads that decides on shared tooling and standards.

## Project codenames

- **HALO**: our largest client engagement, a data platform migration for a retail group. Project codes start with HALO-.
- **FALCON**: the internal staffing and resource-matching tool, built by the Bench team.
- **MERIDIAN**: the company-wide move from Azure DevOps to Jira, finishing this year.
- **PEBBLE**: the internal design system used across client front-ends.
- **Onboard Buddy**: the AI onboarding assistant in Slack (that's this bot).
