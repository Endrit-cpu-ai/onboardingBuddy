# IT Setup, Laptop and VPN

## Collecting your laptop

IT prepares your laptop before Day 1. Collect it from the IT desk on the 2nd floor of the Skopje office between 09:00 and 10:00 on your first day. Remote joiners receive it by courier the week before they start.

## Accounts and MFA

You will receive your Microsoft 365, Slack and Jira accounts on Day 1. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory: install Microsoft Authenticator on your phone and register it at aka.kestrel.example/mfa before 12:00 on your first day.

## Connecting to the VPN

We use GlobalProtect. The VPN portal address is vpn.kestrel.example. Open GlobalProtect, enter the portal address, and sign in with your Kestrel email and MFA. The VPN is only needed for internal systems (the build servers, the staging databases and the legacy intranet). Slack, email and Jira work without it.
