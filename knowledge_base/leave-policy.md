# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Annual leave allowance

Full-time employees get 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. If you join mid-year, your allowance is pro-rated from your start date. You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March.

## How to book leave

Book all leave in Kestrel People (people.kestrel.example). Go to Time Off, choose the leave type and dates, and submit. Your line manager approves the request. Please give at least two weeks' notice for anything longer than three days. After approval, add an "Out of office" block to your calendar and set your Slack status.

## Sick leave

If you are unwell, message your line manager before 09:30 on the day and log a Sick day in Kestrel People when you are back. For absences longer than three consecutive days, HR will contact you about the documentation needed.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the official calendar of the country in your contract. The current year's list is pinned in the #announcements channel.

## Other leave

Parental leave, bereavement leave and unpaid leave are handled case by case with HR. Contact people@kestrel.example.
