# Manager Guide to Onboarding a New Joiner

## Before Day 1

Confirm the laptop order with IT at least 10 working days before the start date. Pick a Buddy from outside your team and brief them. Raise the Jira access ICR so it is ready by week two.

## Probation concerns

If you have concerns about a new joiner during probation, speak to Elena Trajkovska before the Day 60 check-in. Do not discuss probation concerns in Slack channels.
