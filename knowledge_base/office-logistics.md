# Office Logistics

## Address and access

The Skopje office is at Bulevar Partizanski Odredi 42 (fictional), 2nd and 3rd floors. Pick up your access badge from reception on Day 1; bring photo ID. Lost badges are replaced by Sara Lika for a EUR 10 fee.

## Desk booking

Desks are hot-desks. Book a desk in the Deskly app before you come in. Team zones are marked on the floor plan pinned in #office.

## Parking

There are 12 parking spaces in the underground garage, allocated weekly by request in #office every Friday before 12:00. Street parking nearby uses the city SMS parking system.

## Lunch and kitchen

Fruit and coffee are free. The team lunch is on Thursdays at 12:30; the company pays. Please clean up after yourself in the kitchen.
