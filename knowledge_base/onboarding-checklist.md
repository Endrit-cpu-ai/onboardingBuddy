# New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

## Week 1

| Day | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Collect laptop from IT desk, 2nd floor | New joiner |
| Day 1 | Set up MFA before 12:00 | New joiner |
| Day 1 | Welcome session with HR at 11:00 | HR |
| Day 1 | Join Slack channels: #announcements, #it-help, #office, #random, your team channel | New joiner |
| Day 2 | Coffee with your Buddy | Buddy |
| Day 3 | Complete security awareness training in the LMS | New joiner |
| Day 5 | First 1:1 with line manager | Manager |

## Weeks 2 to 4

| Day | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 10 | Read the Engineering Standards page (engineers only) | New joiner |
| Day 10 | Get added to your team's Jira board via an ICR | Manager |
| Day 15 | Complete GDPR and data protection training | New joiner |
| Day 30 | Day 30 check-in with manager and Day 30 survey | Manager, HR |

## After the first month

Day 60 and Day 90 probation check-ins with your manager. Your Buddy check-ins move from weekly to fortnightly after Day 30.
