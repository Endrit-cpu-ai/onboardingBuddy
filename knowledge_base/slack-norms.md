# Slack Norms

## Channels versus DMs

Ask work questions in the relevant channel rather than DMs, so others can learn from the answer. Use threads to keep channels readable. DMs are fine for personal or sensitive topics.

## Response times

Slack is not expected to be instant. A reply within a few hours during core hours is normal. If something is urgent, say so in the first line, or call.

## Status and notifications

Keep your Slack status up to date (for example, OOO, in a client workshop, focus time). Mute channels you do not need. Nobody is expected to read Slack outside their working hours.

## Useful channels

- #announcements: company news, read-only
- #it-help: IT support
- #office: office logistics
- #random: everything else
- #onboarding-help: where Onboard Buddy escalates questions it cannot answer
